## Channel facts worth keeping here

The channel is **Growing With Jesus**. Search is the only way new viewers
arrive at this subscriber count, so titles must match phrases people
actually type. The miscarriage video is the best performing one on the
channel and the only one whose title matched a real search phrase.

Topics with real demand, pulled from YouTube's own search suggestions:

- how to pray for your future husband
- waiting on God for a husband
- what to do when God is silent
- what to do when God says no
- how to hear God's voice
- how to trust God's timing
- how to forgive yourself
- how to stop comparing yourself to others as a Christian
- how to pray when you don't know what to say
- how to stop sinning over and over again

Also viable: church hurt, christian morning routines, how to grow closer
to God every day.

The series name is still unsettled. It is the one line repeated in every
video and every Short, and it is the answer to "what do I get if I
subscribe". Candidates raised so far: Growing Pains, The Part I Don't
Post, The Honest Part, What I Got Wrong.
